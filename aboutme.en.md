This is where I keep the inspiring stories I've read and want to share again.

Every post has a comment section below (via Facebook) for open discussion.

## Self-assessment

Inspiring currently organizes posts by topic and series, with a search page,
topic filters, series filters, and previous–next navigation. Posts in the
same series are numbered clearly so readers can follow from start to finish.

### Strengths

- Diverse content: stories of resilience, kindness, history, and quiet
  reflections on life.
- History posts include info tables, illustrations, and references.
- Cover and thumbnail images are stored on the site to avoid broken images
  from external domains.
- The interface offers multiple ways to browse, search, and group posts by
  series.
- The site checks its build, images, `alt` text, and series ordering before
  deploying.

### Still improving

Some posts still need more specific sources and deeper editing to avoid
overly general conclusions. Images will also keep being reviewed to make
sure they fit the content, are good quality, and credit their sources
clearly.

If I had to score it against the goal of a website sharing inspiring
stories, Inspiring currently sits around **8.7/10**. That number isn't a
final verdict — it's a reminder to keep improving the content, the imagery,
and the reading experience.

What matters most is that each post makes readers pause for a moment,
understand one more story, and find one more reason to live kindly,
persevere, or cherish peace.
