## About this website
{: #about-site-title }

Inspiring is a personal website, created to keep and share inspiring
stories, posts about history, and some reflections on everyday life.

This site **does not operate for commercial purposes**. It sells no
products, offers no paid services, and does not aim to make money from its
content. Every post here exists only to share information, spark thought,
and give readers one more perspective on the story being told.

</section>

<section class="privacy-section" id="personal-data" aria-labelledby="personal-data-title" markdown="1">

## Personal data and privacy
{: #personal-data-title }

This website does not actively collect, buy, sell, or store readers'
personal information. It also does not use cookies for ad tracking or to
build user profiles.

Some embedded content — such as YouTube videos or the Facebook comment
section (when used) — is provided by external platforms. When you interact
with that content, the corresponding platform may process data in its own
way. The website does not control or store any of that data.

</section>

<section class="privacy-section" id="content" aria-labelledby="content-title" markdown="1">

## Nature of the content
{: #content-title }

Posts are written from books, documents, public sources, archival images,
or stories that have been shared. Content is written to be accessible to
readers — it is not a complete research work and does not replace
professional references.

For historical topics, some details may be summarized to suit reading on
the web. When you need to dig deeper, please cross-check with books,
archives, and other reliable sources.

</section>

<section class="privacy-section" id="corrections" aria-labelledby="corrections-title" markdown="1">

## When you spot an error
{: #corrections-title }

I always try to verify information before publishing, but the site may
still contain mistakes in dates, names, places, wording, or references. If
you find something inaccurate, missing context, or needing additions,
please [contact me]({{ '/contact/' | relative_url }}) with the details and
references if available.

After verifying, I will correct, add, or annotate the content as needed.
Important changes may be updated directly in the post so future readers
don't keep hitting inaccurate information.

</section>

<section class="privacy-section" id="sharing" aria-labelledby="sharing-title" markdown="1">

## Sharing the content
{: #sharing-title }

You're welcome to recommend or share links to posts you find useful. When
quoting a passage, please credit **Inspiring** and link back to the
original post so readers can see the full context.

</section>
